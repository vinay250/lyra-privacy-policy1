Privacy Policy for LYRA

Last updated: January 2026

LYRA ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application LYRA collects, uses, and protects user information.

1. Information We Collect

LYRA may access the following permissions only to provide app functionality:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used for voice commands and voice assistant features.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used only when the user activates camera-based features.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used for location-based services such as weather information.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to fetch online content and services.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to show important app notifications.
  • Foreground Service & Wake Lock: Used to keep essential services running properly.
  • Advertising ID & Install Referrer: Used only for basic analytics and app improvement.

LYRA does not collect personally identifiable information such as name, address, or phone number.

2. How We Use Information

The data accessed by LYRA is used strictly to:

  • Enable voice assistant functionality
  • Provide app features requested by the user
  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Deliver notifications and updates

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share user data with third parties.

Any system or service permissions are used only within the app and not for external data distribution.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data. Information processed by the app is handled securely and is not stored permanently unless required for core functionality.

5. Children’s Privacy

LYRA is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us so we can remove it.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
