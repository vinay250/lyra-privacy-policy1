Privacy Policy for LYRA

Last updated: January 2026

LYRA ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the LYRA mobile application collects, uses, and protects user information.

1. Information We Access

LYRA may request access to the following permissions strictly to provide app functionality:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used for voice command features.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used only when the user activates camera-based features.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used to provide location-based services such as weather information.
  • Internet & Network Access: Used to fetch online content such as weather data.
  • Notifications: Used to display important app notifications.
  • Foreground Service & Wake Lock: Used to keep essential services running properly.

LYRA does not collect or store personally identifiable information such as name, address, phone number, or email address.

2. How We Use Information

  • Enable voice assistant functionality
  • Provide weather and other location-based services
  • Deliver requested app features
  • Maintain app performance and stability

3. Data Sharing

LYRA does not sell or rent user data.

Location data may be transmitted securely to trusted third-party weather service providers solely to provide weather functionality. No data is stored permanently by the app.

All data is processed only as necessary to deliver requested features.

4. Data Security

All data transmitted by LYRA is encrypted in transit using secure HTTPS connections.

Information is processed temporarily in memory and is not stored permanently unless required for core functionality.

5. Children’s Privacy

LYRA is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted with a revised date.
